# AutoEnchanter 1.6.0 — Smart Customer Protection

Target: WoW Forever, interface 16001. Release candidate for live client acceptance.

## Added

- 30-minute decline and no-trade departure cooldowns; five verified declines with zero acceptances trigger 30 days, subsequent declines 10 days until acceptance. No permanent decline blacklist.
- Non-tipping restrictions: two distinct unpaid local enchanting dates and zero lifetime tips trigger 30 days; later new unpaid visits 90, 180 and repeated 180 days. Offense history persists. Positive lifetime tips, free guild services and uncertain evidence protected.
- Separate native already-grouped and busy/pending outcomes, independent from declines. Confirmed sent timeouts count as declines; ambiguous timeouts do not. Own pending requests suppressed.
- Cached guild GUID roster refreshed on login and membership events; departed guild members removed after complete refresh. Guild members exempt from non-tipping policy, not decline/no-trade rules. Editable private invitation-time whisper; original default wording; blank or whitespace-only means no whisper. Normal configured party/raid welcome preserved. Whisper follows native invite; native restrictions respected.
- Blacklist tab with search/sort/filter, expiration and offense history, confirmed manual removal; Guild settings tab; grouped/busy customer columns and additional counters.
- Local latency measurements with mean/max/p95/p99 and actual mocked invite-path benchmarks at 1k/10k/50k customers.

## Changed

Schema 2 migrates existing records without deleting whitelist, settings, tips or history. No retroactive bans from legacy ambiguous expirations or unpaid services. Cached customer totals and pending GUID index avoid history scans before invite. Chat-cache pruning moved after invite. Four dedicated modules, expanded policy tests, docs, and version notes. No changes to minimap interactions, welcome configuration, marker or raid rules.

## Upgrade

Refresh WowUp, install, restart WoW. Open /ae settings > Guild settings to edit or disable the private whisper; save an empty field to suppress sending. /ae blacklist opens restrictions. /ae performance reports local processing times. /ae ledgerstatus and /ae diagnostics report policy and guild status. All policy rules apply automatically to real AE requests. Debug creates no real invites or simulated customer offenses.

## Limitations

No claim of live client performance or guaranteed whisper delivery. Named result parsing depends on actual Forever localized messages. Unnamed async results are not guessed; ambiguous sent evidence invalidated. Native trade completion evidence must be verified live before relying on financial penalties. Unverified trades cause conservative under-enforcement. Guild roster unknown means no tip penalty; full snapshot must include player's GUID. Existing v1.5.0 history is preserved, but legacy ambiguous expired rows excluded from verified declines. Five-second settlement protects delayed same-visit payments; uncertain restored/offline closures not punished. Manual removal retains offense history. Use live checklist and inspect CI logs for exact test/benchmark results.
